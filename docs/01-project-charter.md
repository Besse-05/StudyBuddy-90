**One-line description:** StudyBuddy is a web-based platform that helps students find affordable peer tutors, join study groups, share study materials, and organize study sessions in one place.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of StudyBuddy is to give students a single, easy-to-use place to get academic help from their peers. Today, students look for tutors in chat groups, find study partners by word of mouth, and share notes through scattered channels. StudyBuddy brings all of this together.

## 3. Problem Statement

Students face several problems when they need academic support:

- Professional tutoring is expensive, and affordable peer tutors are hard to find.
- There is no central place to see which tutors are available, what they teach, and what they charge.
- Students struggle to find classmates studying the same subject to form or join study groups.
- Notes and study materials are scattered across messaging apps, cloud drives, and personal devices, and are hard to search.
- Scheduling sessions and sending reminders is done manually, so sessions are often missed or forgotten.

## 4. Project Objectives

1. Let students find and request peer tutors by subject, with visible ratings, prices, and availability.
2. Let students search for, join, and leave study groups by subject.
3. Provide a shared library where students can upload, browse, and search study materials.
4. Provide a schedule and chat feature with reminders so sessions are well organized.
5. Deliver a working MVP (Minimum Viable Product) within the course timeline using Agile practices.

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 In Scope (MVP)

**Feature 1: Find a Tutor**
- Browse tutors by subject
- View tutor profiles, subjects, ratings, and prices
- Check tutor availability
- Request a tutoring session

**Feature 2: Study Groups**
- Search for study groups by subject
- View group details, members, and meeting times
- Join or leave a study group
- Connect with other students studying the same subject

**Feature 3: Study Materials**
- Browse notes and study materials by subject
- Upload and share notes
- Search for specific materials
- Access materials for exam preparation

**Feature 4: Schedule & Chat**
- View upcoming tutoring sessions and study groups
- Schedule study sessions
- Chat with tutors and group members
- Receive reminders for upcoming sessions

### 5.2 Out of Scope (for the MVP)

- Online payment processing (prices are shown, payment is arranged directly between student and tutor)
- Built-in video calling
- Native mobile apps (the MVP is a responsive web app)
- Advanced AI recommendations
- Integration with the university's official systems

## 9. Assumptions

- Students have access to a device with a web browser and an internet connection.
- Enough students are willing to register as tutors to make the platform useful.
- Users register with a valid student email address.

## 11. Risks and Mitigation

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Too few tutors join the platform | Medium | High | Invite students with strong grades; offer free tutor listing |
| R2 | Low quality or inappropriate uploaded materials | Medium | Medium | Add report button and admin moderation |
| R3 | Scheduling conflicts between tutors and students | High | Medium | Show availability slots and send reminders |
| R4 | Privacy concerns about personal data and chat | Medium | High | Collect only necessary data; secure passwords; role-based access |
| R5 | Team members fall behind on tasks | Medium | Medium | Short weekly meetings; shared task board; clear ownership |
| R6 | Merge conflicts when combining individual work | Medium | Low | Use GitHub; agree on folder structure and naming |
